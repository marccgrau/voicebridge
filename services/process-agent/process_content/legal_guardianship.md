# Legal Guardianship or Custodianship

Handle notification of legal guardianship or custodianship for a customer's account.

## Step 1: Acknowledge Notification

Thank the caller for notifying the bank and explain the procedure.

## Step 2: Freeze the Account Immediately

On first notification:
- Immediately **freeze the account**
- Prevent all transactions until guardianship is verified
- Ensure account security

## Step 3: Request Written Proof

Request official documentation to be sent:
- Court order appointing the guardian
- Legal guardianship certificate
- Power of attorney documents
- Any other relevant legal proof

Provide the address or method for submitting documentation.

## Step 4: Explain the Verification Process

Inform the caller:
- Documents will be reviewed by legal department
- Verification may take 3-5 business days
- Account remains frozen during review

## Step 5: Update Records and Unfreeze

Once proof is received and verified:
- Update account records with guardian information
- Add guardian to authorized user list
- Unfreeze the account
- Notify the guardian that account access is restored

## Step 6: Handle Guardian Changes

If the guardian changes in the future:
- Repeat the entire process
- Freeze account until new guardianship is verified
- Update all records accordingly
