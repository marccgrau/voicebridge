# E-Banking Login Locked

Customer cannot access e-banking due to too many failed login attempts.

## Step 1: Verify Identity

Complete identity verification using name, date of birth, and registered address.

## Step 2: Unlock the Account

Unlock the contract in the core system to restore access.

## Step 3: Choose Password Reset Method

Offer the customer two options:

**Default (Recommended):** SMS one-time code
- Arrives within 10 minutes
- Free of charge
- Immediate access

**Alternative:** Postal starter-password letter
- Delivery time: approximately 2 business days
- Free of charge
- More secure but slower

## Step 4: Process the Reset

Execute the chosen reset method and confirm with the customer.

## Step 5: Provide Instructions

Remind the customer to:
- Change the initial password at first login
- Choose a strong, unique password
- Keep their login credentials secure

## Step 6: Close with Recap

Provide a friendly recap of the reset method chosen and offer further assistance.
