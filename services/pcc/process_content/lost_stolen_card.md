# Lost or Stolen Card

## Step 1: Verify Identity

Complete identity verification before proceeding with card blocking.

## Step 2: Confirm Card Type

Confirm which card type is affected:
- Debit card
- Credit card
- Both cards

## Step 3: Block the Card Immediately

Permanently block the affected card(s).
- Do **not** use temporary blocks for lost/stolen cards
- Reassure the customer that no further transactions are possible

## Step 4: Offer Replacement Card

Ask whether the customer would like a replacement card sent.
- Default delivery: registered home address
- Alternative: any branch or temporary foreign address

## Step 5: Quote Delivery Times

Inform the customer of standard delivery times:
- Debit card: 3-5 business days
- Credit card: 6 business days (shipped by card partner)
- Replacement fee: CHF 30 (flat fee)
- PIN stays the same by default (can be changed on request)

## Step 6: Confirm and Close

Place the replacement order, confirm details with the customer, and offer SMS notification service for delivery status.
