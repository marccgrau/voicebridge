# Small Estates (Under CHF 20,000)

Process for releasing funds from small estates without requiring probate.

## Step 1: Confirm Estate Value

Verify that the total estate value is under CHF 20,000.

## Step 2: Offer Letter of Indemnity

Explain the letter of indemnity option:
- Protects the bank from financial loss
- Allows funds to be released without probate
- Faster than the standard probate process

## Step 3: Explain Liability Waiver

Inform the heirs about:
- Liability waiver requirements
- Potential risks to heirs if claims arise later
- Legal implications of signing the indemnity

## Step 4: Collect Heir Information

Gather information about all heirs:
- Full names
- Contact details
- Relationship to deceased
- Share of inheritance

## Step 5: Send Letter of Indemnity

Send the letter of indemnity to all identified heirs for signature.

## Step 6: Process Payout Upon All Signatures

Explain that:
- Payout or account closure occurs **only when all heirs have signed**
- No partial payouts are allowed
- Funds remain frozen until all signatures are received

## Step 7: Execute Payout or Close Account

Once all signatures are received:
- Distribute funds according to legal share
- Close the account
- Provide confirmation to all heirs
