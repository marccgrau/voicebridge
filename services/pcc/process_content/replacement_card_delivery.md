# Replacement Card & Delivery

Customer needs a replacement card (following loss, theft, damage, or expiration).

## Step 1: Confirm Delivery Address

Ask where the replacement card should be sent:

**Default option:** Registered home address
**Alternative options:**
- Any branch location (for pick-up)
- Temporary foreign address (if applicable)

## Step 2: Quote Delivery Times

Inform the customer of standard delivery timeframes:
- **Debit card:** 3-5 business days
- **Credit card:** 6 business days (shipped by card partner)
- Note: No expedited delivery is available

## Step 3: Explain Fees and PIN Policy

Inform the customer:
- Replacement card fee: **CHF 30** (flat fee)
- PIN stays the same by default
- PIN can be changed or reset upon request

## Step 4: Place the Replacement Order

Process the replacement card order in the system and confirm the details with the customer.

## Step 5: Offer SMS Notification

Ask if the customer would like SMS notifications about the delivery status (optional, free service).

## Step 6: Confirm and Close

Summarize:
- Card type being replaced
- Delivery address
- Expected delivery time
- Total fee
- SMS notification preference (if applicable)
