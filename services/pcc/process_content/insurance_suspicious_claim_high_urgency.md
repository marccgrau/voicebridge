# Insurance Suspicious Claim Activity (High Urgency)

Use this process when a policyholder reports a potentially fraudulent claim and requests immediate protection.

## Step 1: Opening Alert

Confirm suspicious claim activity and acknowledge urgency.

## Step 2: Identity Verification

Verify policyholder identity before discussing claim details.

## Step 3: Immediate Security Action

Apply immediate policy protection controls according to fraud policy.

## Step 4: Recent Activity Capture

Capture timeline, suspicious claim details, and customer-reported mismatches.

## Step 5: Dispute Case Initiation

Open the fraud dispute or investigation workflow with reference details.

## Step 6: Downstream Containment Action

Apply temporary watch or protective note to reduce additional misuse risk.

## Step 7: Preference Capture

Confirm customer communication preferences for updates.

## Step 8: Boundary Challenge

Handle requests for guaranteed same-day final resolution.

## Step 9: Policy-Consistent Response

Provide realistic review constraints and immediate safeguards.

## Step 10: Notifications and Tracking

Confirm alert channels and when updates are triggered.

## Step 11: Final Clarification

Provide contingency guidance for any new suspicious activity while review is pending.

## Step 12: Structured Close

Recap protections, investigation status, timeline, and customer action points.
