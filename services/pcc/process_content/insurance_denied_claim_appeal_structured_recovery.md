# Insurance Denied Claim Appeal (Structured Recovery)

Use this process when a policyholder challenges a denied claim and requests a clear appeal path.

## Step 1: Opening Complaint

Acknowledge concern and confirm denial context.

## Step 2: Identity Verification

Verify policyholder identity before discussing protected claim details.

## Step 3: Decision Explanation

Explain reason code and policy basis in plain language.

## Step 4: Gap Diagnosis

Identify missing evidence, eligibility criteria, or rule mismatch.

## Step 5: Reconsideration Path Offer

Offer appeal route and prerequisites.

## Step 6: Evidence Collection Guidance

Provide exact document checklist, validity expectations, and quality thresholds.

## Step 7: Submission Method Selection

Explain accepted submission channels and recommend the fastest reliable one.

## Step 8: Interim Support Request

Handle customer request for interim support while appeal is pending.

## Step 9: Escalation or Interim Option

Provide policy-compliant interim support boundaries.

## Step 10: Timeline and SLA Communication

Set realistic review timeline with milestone communication expectations.

## Step 11: Communication Preference

Confirm preferred notification channel.

## Step 12: Structured Close

Recap required evidence, submission path, timeline, and follow-up triggers.
