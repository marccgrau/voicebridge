# Multiple Cards Lost Abroad

Customer has lost or had stolen multiple cards while traveling abroad.

## Step 1: Express Concern and Verify Identity

Show empathy for the difficult situation and complete identity verification.

## Step 2: Identify All Affected Cards

Confirm which cards are affected:
- Debit card(s)
- Credit card(s)
- Any additional payment cards

## Step 3: Block All Cards Immediately

Permanently block **all affected cards**:
- Debit cards
- Credit cards
- Reassure customer that no further transactions are possible on blocked cards

## Step 4: Discuss Delivery Preferences

For each replacement card, ask about delivery preference:
- **Home address** (registered address)
- **Branch pick-up** (any branch location)
- Note: Cards cannot be sent to foreign addresses while customer is traveling

Inform about standard delivery times:
- Debit card: 3-5 business days
- Credit card: 6 business days

## Step 5: Explain Emergency Cash Options

Inform about emergency cash withdrawal options:
- Available through credit card partner
- Emergency cash fee: **CHF 2.5%** of withdrawal amount
- Available at partner bank locations abroad
- Requires identity verification

## Step 6: Process Replacement Orders

Place orders for all replacement cards with chosen delivery methods.

## Step 7: Provide Emergency Support Information

Give the customer:
- Emergency contact numbers (available 24/7)
- Credit card partner emergency line
- Instructions for emergency cash pickup
- Confirmation of blocked cards

## Step 8: Summarize and Confirm

Recap all actions taken:
- Cards blocked
- Replacement cards ordered
- Delivery methods chosen
- Emergency cash options explained
- Total replacement fees (CHF 30 per card)

Offer additional assistance and ensure customer has all necessary information.
