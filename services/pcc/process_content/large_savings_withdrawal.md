# Large Savings Withdrawal

Customer wants to withdraw an amount exceeding the monthly withdrawal limit.

## Step 1: Identify the Request

Confirm the withdrawal amount and verify it exceeds the monthly limit (e.g., CHF 50,000).

## Step 2: Explain the Rules

Inform the customer:
- Amounts above the monthly withdrawal limit require advance **notice**
- Minimum notice period: 31 days
- Notice remains valid for 60 days from the chosen start date
- Withdrawals above the limit without notice incur a Non-Notice Commission (~2%)

## Step 3: Present Customer Options

Explain two available options:

| Option | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| File a notice today (free) | Full amount available after 31-60 days |
| Immediate payout | Bank charges Non-Notice Commission (~2%) on excess amount |

## Step 4: Warn About Online Withdrawals

Warn that exceeding the limit via e-banking will trigger the same fee automatically (fee is shown in the online interface before confirmation).

## Step 5: Record Customer Choice

Document the customer's decision:
- If filing notice: Set the notice period and confirm the availability date
- If immediate payout: Calculate the fee, confirm the total cost, and execute the transfer

## Step 6: Confirm and Close

Summarize the action taken, confirm any fees or waiting periods, and ensure the customer understands the next steps.
