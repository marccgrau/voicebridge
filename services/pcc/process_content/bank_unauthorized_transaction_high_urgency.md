# Bank Unauthorized Transaction (High Urgency)

Use this process when the caller reports suspicious card activity and needs immediate containment.

## Step 1: Opening Alert

Recognize urgency and confirm the customer is reporting an unauthorized card transaction.

## Step 2: Identity Verification

Complete required identity verification before any account-level action.

## Step 3: Immediate Security Action

Block or freeze the compromised card according to policy.

## Step 4: Recent Activity Capture

Capture the last legitimate usage and suspicious transaction details (time, amount, merchant).

## Step 5: Dispute Case Initiation

Open a formal dispute/fraud case and set expectations on investigation flow.

## Step 6: Continuity Action

Offer replacement card ordering to restore payment continuity.

## Step 7: Preference Capture

Confirm delivery preference and practical constraints.

## Step 8: Boundary Challenge

Handle requests for guaranteed instant delivery under urgency.

## Step 9: Policy-Consistent Response

Set realistic constraints, avoid overpromising, and offer the best available alternative.

## Step 10: Notifications and Tracking

Confirm the customer update channel and milestone notifications.

## Step 11: Final Clarification

Clarify credential continuity questions (for example PIN behavior and reset options).

## Step 12: Structured Close

Recap security action, dispute status, replacement path, timeline, and customer next steps.
