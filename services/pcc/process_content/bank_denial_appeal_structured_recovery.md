# Bank Denial Appeal (Structured Recovery)

Use this process when the customer challenges a denial and requests reconsideration with clear next steps.

## Step 1: Opening Complaint

Acknowledge dissatisfaction and confirm the denial concern.

## Step 2: Identity Verification

Complete standard verification before discussing case details.

## Step 3: Decision Explanation

Explain denial basis in plain language with policy context.

## Step 4: Gap Diagnosis

Identify missing criteria, thresholds, or required evidence.

## Step 5: Reconsideration Path Offer

Offer the valid appeal path and prerequisites.

## Step 6: Evidence Collection Guidance

Provide exact document checklist and acceptance criteria.

## Step 7: Submission Method Selection

Present accepted submission channels and recommend the fastest reliable route.

## Step 8: Interim Support Request

Handle what can be offered while the appeal is under review.

## Step 9: Escalation or Interim Option

Provide policy-compliant interim options without overpromising.

## Step 10: Timeline and SLA Communication

Set realistic review timeline and milestone expectations.

## Step 11: Communication Preference

Confirm update channel and contact expectations.

## Step 12: Structured Close

Recap submission requirements, timeline, and follow-up trigger points.
