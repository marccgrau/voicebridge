# Reporting a Death

Handle the notification of a customer's death with sensitivity and professionalism.

## Step 1: Express Condolences

Express sincere condolences to the caller and acknowledge the difficulty of the situation.

## Step 2: Freeze Customer Relationship

Immediately freeze the customer relationship in the system with status "estate liquidation."

## Step 3: Block All Access

Take immediate actions to secure the account:
- Block all debit and credit cards of the deceased
- Disable e-banking access
- Prevent any new transactions

## Step 4: Explain Required Documentation

Inform the caller about the documents needed to proceed:
- Death certificate (certified copy)
- Proof of probate or executor appointment (if applicable)
- Identification of heirs

## Step 5: Inform About Small Estates Process

If the estate is under CHF 20,000:
- Offer a **letter of indemnity** option
- Explain that this allows fund release without probate
- Mention liability waiver and potential risks to heirs
- Note that payout or account closure requires **all heirs to sign**

## Step 6: Explain Payout Timeline

Inform heirs about the 31-day notice period for payouts from savings accounts.

## Step 7: Provide Next Steps

Summarize:
- Documents required
- Where to send documentation
- Expected timeline
- Contact information for follow-up questions
- Offer assistance and express support during this difficult time
